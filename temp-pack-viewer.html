Known Issues with Datamuse Slant Rhymes:
The rel_nry (near rhyme) endpoint returns words with similar sounds, but they often don't "slant rhyme" in the hip-hop sense. For example, "word" returns "wad", "weed", "wide" which just share the "w" sound.
